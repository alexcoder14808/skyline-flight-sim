About

Skyline Flight is a from-scratch, browser-based 3D flight simulator built with Three.js and WebGL. This is V1: a complete playable foundation (real flight physics, a full airport and terrain, day/night, weather, HUD/cockpit, and audio) designed to be expanded with more aircraft, real-world terrain, navigation, and multiplayer in later versions.
